01 / COMMUNITY GRANT

Minor Rural Project (MRP)

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT

The Minor Rural Project (MRP) is an ongoing Sarawak Government grant initiative that supports community development in Sebauh and the wider Kemena constituency. Grants are channelled to village and longhouse committees, NGOs, youth associations, schools and places of worship. The funding supports social programmes, community activities and small-scale improvements to local facilities. Allocations are distributed in phases throughout the year.

26 JUNE 2026

MRP Phase Two allocation

Community recipients in the Kemena constituency received MRP Phase Two allocations to support local programmes and community needs.

02 / PLACE OF WORSHIP

St. George Church Sebauh

CONSTRUCTION

St. George Church is a development project located at Lot 969, Block 8, Selezu Land District, Sebauh, Bintulu. The project includes the main church building, priest residence, catechist house and supporting facilities. Construction updates cover building works and external infrastructure. 03 / PLACE OF WORSHIP

New Sebauh Mosque

CONSTRUCTION

The New Sebauh Mosque is a development project located on part of Lot 846, Block 8, Selezu Land District, Sebauh Town, Bintulu. It is intended to provide a place of worship and community gathering for local residents. The project’s development is documented through site visits, construction milestones and photographs.

04 / PUBLIC AMENITY

Sebauh Waterfront

PROPOSED UPGRADING

The Proposed Upgrading of Sebauh Waterfront is a development initiative aimed at improving the functionality and appearance of the town’s riverside area. The project is planned to enhance public spaces and provide greater capacity for community activities and public events. The proposed works are intended to create a more modern, inclusive and sustainable waterfront attraction. The contract is scheduled to commence in the fourth quarter of 2026.
